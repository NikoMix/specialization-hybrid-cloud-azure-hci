The guide is organised the way the audit is run. **Pre-qualification** is the
gate you clear before requesting the audit. **Module A** covers the general
organisational controls; **Module B** covers the Azure Local specific controls
and carries the technical evidence. The **engagement playbook** is how you build
the delivery capability that produces that evidence in the first place.
