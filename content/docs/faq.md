## Pre-Qualification

### How do I know if we already meet the ACR thresholds?

Check **Partner Center → Insights → Azure Revenue** and filter by the spec's measurement window (typically the last 12 months for Infrastructure-track specializations — `TODO: verify against spec PDF`). Note that Partner Center data typically lags by **2–4 weeks**, so plan accordingly. Your PDM can provide a more current view on request.

### Can we count ACR from multiple subscription types (CSP, DPOR, PAL)?

Yes. All three eligible association types count: **Digital Partner of Record (DPOR)**, **Partner Admin Link (PAL)**, and **Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)**. A single customer may have multiple association types, but only counts as one unique customer for the diversity requirement.

### Does Azure Stack HCI consumption count? What about the rebrand to Azure Local?

Yes — **Azure Local** is the current name for what was previously **Azure Stack HCI**. Billing meters and ACR continue to flow under the same Microsoft commerce SKUs. Existing Azure Stack HCI deployments count toward this specialization's ACR requirement.

### Do we need AZ-800 AND AZ-801, or just one?

The **Windows Server Hybrid Administrator Associate** badge requires both AZ-800 and AZ-801 to be passed by the same individual. Most spec requirements call out the *associate* badge rather than the individual exam, so both are typically needed.

## Audit Process

### How long does the audit take?

For well-prepared partners, the full process from audit request to badge publication is typically **6–8 weeks**. Unprepared partners who need to gather evidence during the audit process typically take **12–16 weeks**.

### What format does the auditor want evidence in?

Auditors generally accept **PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint** documents. Screenshots should be exported as PDF where possible. All documents should be clearly named with control references (e.g., `A.2.1_QMS_Policy_v2.pdf`).

### What happens if we fail the audit?

You will receive a **remediation report** itemising the specific controls and gaps. You have approximately **30 days** to address and resubmit findings. If you fail to remediate in time, you may need to restart the audit process. There is typically a waiting period before requesting a new audit.

### Can we anonymise customer names in case studies and references?

Yes, for internal audit documents. However, the auditor may verify customer references directly with Microsoft Partner Center data. Work with your PDM to understand what level of anonymisation is acceptable.

### Is the audit conducted in English?

The audit is conducted in **English** by default. Some audit firms may offer interviews in other languages — confirm with your assigned auditor at kickoff.

## Evidence

### What counts as proof of an Azure Local cluster deployment?

Strong evidence typically includes:
- Cluster registration confirmation in Azure (showing the Azure Local resource and HCI billing meter active)
- A bill of materials (BoM) for the validated hardware platform (Dell, HPE, Lenovo, etc.)
- High-level architecture diagram showing nodes, network design (storage/management/compute VLANs), Arc enrolment, and connected Azure services (Monitor, Backup, Site Recovery)
- A deployment runbook or as-built document (anonymised) showing the configuration steps that were executed
- Customer sign-off on go-live

### What counts as proof of a customer satisfaction survey?

Accepted formats include:
- Exported survey results from tools like Microsoft Forms, SurveyMonkey, or Qualtrics
- CSAT data from your CRM (anonymised if needed)
- Email correspondence confirming customer satisfaction scores
- Reference letters from customers

The auditor is looking for a **systematic process**, not just a one-off survey.

### Do we need ISO 27001 for the security control?

ISO 27001 is the gold standard and will satisfy the security control. However, **equivalent evidence** is accepted, including:
- SOC 2 Type II report
- Internal information security policy documented to a recognised framework (NIST, CIS)
- Evidence that the policy is actively maintained and communicated to staff

### What if a certification is expiring soon?

Certifications must be **active at the time of audit submission**. If a certification expires during the audit process, have the individual renew it. Microsoft certifications typically have a **2-year validity** and can be renewed via the Microsoft Learn renewal exam (no fee for renewal exams).

## Post-Specialization

### How long is the Advanced Specialization valid?

Advanced Specializations are valid for **one year** and must be renewed annually. The renewal process includes a re-audit or a lighter evidence update, depending on Microsoft's renewal policy at the time.

### Where does the specialization badge appear?

The badge appears on your **Microsoft AppSource partner profile**, in **Microsoft Partner Finder**, and can be downloaded from Partner Center for use on your website and marketing materials.

### Are there additional benefits beyond the badge?

Yes. Benefits typically include:
- **Co-sell priority** — Microsoft field sellers see you as a qualified partner for Azure Local / hybrid infrastructure workloads
- **Marketing through Microsoft** (MtM) eligibility
- **Partner-funded marketing** opportunities
- Access to **Microsoft-led go-to-market programmes**
