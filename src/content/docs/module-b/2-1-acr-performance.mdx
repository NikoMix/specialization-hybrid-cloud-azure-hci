## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation generates the required **Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR)** from the eligible Azure Local and hybrid infrastructure services over the spec's defined measurement window.

**Typical questions:**
- Can you show Partner Center ACR reports for the measurement window?
- Is the qualifying ACR from eligible services (Azure Local, Arc, hybrid resiliency, AKS hybrid)?
- Are the ACR figures associated via eligible partner types (DPOR, PAL, CSP)?

<Aside type="caution">
  ACR data in Partner Center lags by **2–4 weeks**. Confirm exact figures with your PDM, especially
  if you are close to the threshold.
</Aside>

## How to Export ACR Data from Partner Center

1. Navigate to **Partner Center → Insights → Azure Revenue**
2. Set date range to the spec's measurement window (typically the last 12 full months)
3. Export to Excel / download the report
4. Filter or annotate by service to show eligible-category alignment
5. Highlight the total per category and the overall qualifying total

<Aside type="tip">
  Use the **"Revenue by Customer"** view to identify unique customer contributions simultaneously —
  this saves time when preparing evidence for B.2.2 (Customer Diversity) as well.
</Aside>

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| ACR below threshold for the eligible categories | Discuss with PDM whether any eligible services are miscategorised; consider accelerating customer Azure Local cluster registrations or Arc enrolments |
| Association type missing for some subscriptions | Work with the customer to establish DPOR or PAL links immediately |
| ACR report doesn't show service-level breakdown | Request a detailed report from PDM or use the Insights API |
| Revenue near threshold | Confirm with PDM — Partner Center may lag and actual figures may be higher |
| Customer is on Azure Stack HCI (legacy SKU) and unsure if it still counts | Confirm with PDM — Azure Local is the rebrand; existing HCI billing meters continue to qualify |
